# Squirrel Girl - Custom Marvel Champions Hero Pack

## Complete Design, Squirrel Deck, Obligation, Nemesis Set, and Recommended Starter Deck

---

# 1. Design Overview

Squirrel Girl uses a separate six-card **Squirrel deck**.

Her Squirrel allies are deliberately weak and situational. They can attack, thwart, trigger small abilities, or be spent to improve Squirrel Girl's signature cards.

The central decision is always the same:

1. Use a Squirrel for its printed ability.
2. Keep it in play for later.
3. Spend it to reduce the cost of a SQUIRREL GIRL card.

Squirrel allies do not count against the normal ally limit, cannot defend, and cannot be strengthened by cards outside the SQUIRREL GIRL set.

# 3. Squirrel Deck Rules

During setup, set the six SQUIRREL allies aside and shuffle them to form the **Squirrel deck**.

The Squirrel deck is separate from the player's main deck and does not count toward the player's deck size.

## Calling a Squirrel

When an effect tells you to **call 1 Squirrel**:

1. If the Squirrel deck is empty, shuffle the Squirrel discard pile to create a new Squirrel deck.
2. Reveal the top card of the Squirrel deck.
3. Put that Squirrel ally into play under your control, exhausted.

A card effect can explicitly cause a called Squirrel to enter play ready instead.

## Spending a Squirrel

To **spend a Squirrel**, discard a SQUIRREL ally you control and place it in the Squirrel discard pile.

When you play a SQUIRREL GIRL card, you may spend 1 Squirrel to reduce that card's resource cost by 1.

Only 1 Squirrel can be spent this way for each card played.

Some SQUIRREL GIRL card abilities can also instruct you to spend a Squirrel for another effect.

## Special Rules

SQUIRREL allies:

- Do not count against your ally limit.
- Cannot defend.
- Cannot have upgrades attached to them.
- Cannot be chosen as the target or cost of a player card ability unless that card belongs to the SQUIRREL GIRL set.
- Cannot be readied by player card effects unless that effect belongs to the SQUIRREL GIRL set.
- Cannot have their THW or ATK increased by player card effects unless that effect belongs to the SQUIRREL GIRL set.
- Are placed in the Squirrel discard pile whenever they leave play.

Because the Squirrel deck contains only six cards, no more than six SQUIRREL allies can be in play at the same time.

# 7. Obligation

## Surprise Exam

**Card Type:** Obligation

Give this card to the Doreen Green player.

**When Revealed:** You may flip to alter-ego form. Choose one:

- Exhaust Doreen Green and discard a card with a printed Mental resource from your hand. Remove Surprise Exam from the game.
- Discard up to 2 SQUIRREL allies you control. For each Squirrel you could not discard this way, discard 1 random card from your hand. Shuffle Surprise Exam into the encounter deck.

# 10. Recommended Starter Aspect

## Aggression

Aggression is the recommended starting aspect for Squirrel Girl.

Leadership is thematically tempting, but her Squirrels are already special allies. A Leadership deck could too easily turn them into a generic ally engine.

Aggression supports her intended play pattern more cleanly:

- Find and engage minions.
- Defeat minions with Squirrel Girl's basic attack.
- Trigger Squirrel Girl's Costume.
- Call more Squirrels.
- Ready Squirrel Girl and attack again.
- Use Squirrels as a flexible resource without increasing their printed combat values.

The recommended list assumes access to the card pool available through **Fear No Evil**.

# 13. Recommended Play Pattern

## Early Game

Prioritize cards that improve Squirrel Girl's economy and basic attacks:

1. Squirrel Girl's Costume
2. Prehensile Tail
3. Combat Training
4. Martial Prowess
5. Central Park

Do not play every setup card automatically. Central Park, Network of Trees, and Hall of Heroes are situational investments.

## Mid Game

Use minion-search effects to create targets for Squirrel Girl.

A typical sequence can be:

1. Find or put a minion into play.
2. Defeat it with Squirrel Girl's basic attack.
3. Trigger Squirrel Girl's Costume to call a Squirrel.
4. Trigger Battle Fury or play Spoiling for a Fight to ready Squirrel Girl.
5. Attack again or use the second activation for another objective.

## Squirrel Decisions

For each Squirrel, decide whether to:

- Use its THW or ATK.
- Preserve its printed ability.
- Spend it to reduce the cost of a SQUIRREL GIRL card.

The Squirrels are not intended to become a conventional ally swarm. Their printed values remain fixed unless a SQUIRREL GIRL card explicitly changes them.

# 14. Initial Balance Points to Test

The following elements should receive particular attention during playtesting:

- Whether **Unbeatable Army** should remain limited to once per phase or be reduced to once per round.
- Whether **Call the Squirrels** provides too much immediate value when both Squirrels enter ready.
- Whether **Unbeatable!** creates too many additional hero activations.
- Whether **Nuts!** is appropriately strong as a one-copy signature resource.
- Whether six Squirrels provide enough circulation without making the Squirrel deck feel repetitive.
- Whether **Spoiling for a Fight**, **Battle Fury**, and **Squirrel Girl's Costume** create too many basic attacks in a single round.
- Whether the restriction preventing Squirrel stat increases is sufficient to stop external ally effects from breaking the Squirrel deck.
