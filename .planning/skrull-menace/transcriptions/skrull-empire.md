# Skrull Empire (Skrull Menace Expansion Content) - card transcriptions

Source: `Scenario Packs/Skrull Empire (Skrull Menace Expansion Content)/` of the Kajislav custom content. Set label on every card: SKRULL EMPIRE (n/46). Footer on every card: "MC Custom Content By: Kajislav [icon] <id>" (the id is printed beside the icon; it is the second number given per card as 'Footer id'). Card face is 1500x2150; setup main schemes are landscape.

50 card images transcribed (one section per file): 12 villain stages, 11 setup files (8 main-scheme sides = 4 double-sided cards, + 3 environment stages), 9 planets, 18 encounter cards (duplicates each transcribed). Non-card images skipped: insert.png, insert 2.png, covers, release image.

Legend for the icons as printed: red "Y" trident-like symbol = boost-icon-type pip printed at bottom-right (printed copies counted below); red star = star-shaped boost icon; "house with bar and plus" icon = acceleration icon (confirmed by Wrath of the Empire text); person silhouette = per-player marker.

Note on rules wording: a star (black five-point star) before a rule paragraph is printed on the card as the villain/enemy "hero-version-star" marker; it is reproduced below as the star symbol.

## Mechanics noticed
- Villain deck: a custom deck built at setup ("Create the PLANET and the Villain deck (see insert)", insert.png/insert 2.png not transcribed). Four villains x 3 stages: Emperor Dorrek I, Emperor Dorrek VII, Empress R'Klll, Empress S'Byll. Reveal the top card; Overthrow removes the current villain and reveals the next villain-deck card.
- PLANET deck: a custom deck of 9 planet environments (Location. Planet.). The main scheme B sides reveal the top card of the PLANET deck on each stage change. Planets alter global rules (Skrull minions +hit points / +ATK / +SCH / guard, identities -1 REC, side schemes gain crisis icon, main scheme target threat -1 per player, a card gains amplify icon while a side scheme is in play, allies/supports enter exhausted). The Skrull Empire environment's Incite Rebellion hero action blanks a PLANET's text for the round.
- New trait words: SKRULL (bold italic comic font in rules), PLANET (same), Location, Condition.
- Main scheme A sides are setup/contents cards (Contents, Setup); expert mode replaces stages (I),(II) with (II),(III).
- Keywords seen: Retaliate 1/2, Incite 1 [person]/Incite 2, Steady, Stalwart, Toughness, Overkill, Piercing, Ranged, Guard, Surge. "Overkill" and "Stalwart"/"Steady" as printed.
- Terms: acceleration token / acceleration icon, crisis icon, amplify icon, boost icon (red Y = Skrull set icon doubling as boost icon; red star = star boost icon), target threat value, "set aside, out of play" Overthrow cards, "Treat its printed text box as if it were blank", "deal this card to yourself as a facedown encounter card".
- Villain stages have no hit points printed on the card (likely on the insert or villain deck definition). ATK shown with a star superscript on some stages (1★ 2★ etc.) meaning "star" ATK mark; S'Byll stages have none.
- Hy'lt Minor "Each identity gets -1 REC" (recovery reduction); Aks'Lo typo "spacefor"; Zaragz'na typo "befor".
- Card-count mismatch of the set: 46 cards numbered; numbers 1-46 all covered by the files above (duplicates: 32-34, 35-36, 37-38, 39-40, 41-43, 44-46).
