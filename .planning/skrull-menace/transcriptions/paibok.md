# Paibok scenario pack (Skrull Menace Expansion Content) : transcription

Source: `Scenario Packs/Paibok (Skrull Menace Expansion Content)/`. 30 card files (7 setup files = 5 card numbers, 23 encounter). Footer of every card: "MC Custom Content By: Kajislav" + encounter-set icon + collection number ("Set label"). Number at bottom-left: "PAIBOK (n/28)". Teal/green frame.

Icon legend: physical = fist icon, energy = lightning-bolt icon, mental = small node/brain icon. Boost icons at bottom-right: red star and pink Y (Y = the same "boost icon" shape defined in the Super-Skrull pack's Power Multiplying card). [star] = black star bullet before a rules line.

## Mechanics noticed
- **Mastery counters** (custom counter) on Paibok: placed at Setup (1), by 2A When Revealed, and by 2B Forced Interrupt. While POWER SKRULL attachments on Paibok <= mastery counters, they cannot be discarded (printed on every Paibok villain stage).
- **POWER SKRULL** is a printed trait (special comic font) on 4 attachments: Electricity Projection, Ice Control, Metallic Skin, Shapeshifting. Loss condition on 2B: all 4 POWER SKRULL cards attached to Paibok when the player phase ends. Each is removed by a player Hero Action spending specific resource types (physical/energy/mental pairs, or 3 of any type for Ice Control).
- **Conditions** (Distracted, Hypnotized, Trapped in Ice) attach to the player's identity and are removed by a Hero Action spending 2 specific resources.
- Attachments' **Boost** text is reused as an active effect: Captain Paibok Hero Action, General Kalamari When Defeated, Power Demonstration, Getting Ruthless (Alter-Ego), Mastering Superpowers 2A, Settling Scores (each boost card resolves its Boost 1 additional time). The word "Boost" is quoted in bold.
- Title attachment (Captain Paibok) found by Mastering Superpowers 2A.
- "When Revealed (Alter-Ego)" / "When Revealed (Hero)" split on Getting Ruthless.
- Settling Scores uses Hinder 2 with per-player scaling.
- Star-bullet [star] lines are used as "star" abilities on Ice Control, Captain Paibok boost etc.; 5B has a star next to the threat value (mod: threat 5 star per player).
- Setup contents: Paibok I + II (II + III for expert), Paibok + Standard encounter sets, modular set New Revengers.
- Player-count-scaled hit points on villain stages (13/16/20).
