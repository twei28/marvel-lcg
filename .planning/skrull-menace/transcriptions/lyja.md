## Mechanics noticed
- Treachery as a player card type/trait: Lyja's deck contains **TREACHERY** events with "Hero Action: Shuffle this card into the encounter deck", "When Revealed" and a "★ Boost" (player cards carrying When Revealed / Boost text and a boost line separated by a rule). Cards 02-09 have a red star badge next to the set label (meaning unknown; probably marks the encounter-deck-shuffled cards).
- Hero/identity puts player cards into the encounter deck and tutors **TREACHERY** cards from it ("Skrull Spy", Human Torch, Playing Along, etc.), including placing them on top in any order.
- Boost-star icon rules: "★" icon in the boost area is referenced by Ministerial Tiara (interrupt: draw when a ★ boost card is turned up) and Hit the Weak Spot (deal damage per ★ and Y-icon in a minion's boost area; attack gains piercing).
- New boost icon: a red Y-shaped icon ("Y-icon") appears on all Lyja encounter cards (1 or 2 per card) and is counted by Hit the Weak Spot; no rules text for it on these cards.
- New counter: "revelation counter" (Invisible Woman) with consequential damage scaling.
- Consequential-damage icon (hollow star) below ally THW/ATK; "Toughness" keyword and tough status card (The Thing, Paibok).
- Team-Up (Human Torch and Lyja), Max 1 per deck (Laser Tag).
- "Hero Resource", "Hero Interrupt", "Hero Response", "Alter-Ego Action", "Alter-Ego Response" timing prefixes; "Forced Response", "Forced Interrupt".
- Nemesis set of 5 (Paibok minion, Punish the Defector side scheme, 2 copies of "It's a Stinkin' Skrull!", Truth Revealed) with an Obligation "Untrustworthiness" given to the Laura Green player ("deal yourself 1 facedown encounter card", i.e. dealing facedown encounter cards).
- Fantastic Four Member title upgrade lets a character gain a trait; Mole title upgrade resolves another card's When Revealed from the top-of-deck discard.
- Side scheme 43 carries a crisis-style icon (cross in rounded square) in its text area.

## Illegible / uncertain
- The red star badge meaning and the exact identity of the red Y-icon and the white cross icon are inferred, not printed in words.
- The exact name of the encounter card set (card 41 set label is just "LYJA").
