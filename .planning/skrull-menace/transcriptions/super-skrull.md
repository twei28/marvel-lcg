# Super-Skrull scenario pack (Skrull Menace Expansion Content) : transcription

Source: `Scenario Packs/Super-Skrull (Skrull Menace Expansion Content)/`. 29 cards (7 setup files = 5 card numbers, 18 encounter, 4 superpower). Footer of every card: "MC Custom Content By: Kajislav" + encounter-set icon + a collection number (given as "Set label" below). Number at bottom-left is "SUPER-SKRULL (n/27)". Green frame.

Icon legend used below (as printed): physical resource = fist icon, energy = lightning-bolt icon, mental = small brain/node icon, wild = star-burst icon. "Boost icon (red star)" = red star at bottom-right. "Y boost icon" = pink Y-shaped icon at bottom-right (the card text of Power Multiplying calls it "boost icon (Y)"). The small pink Y also appears as the encounter-set symbol, see Mechanics.

## Mechanics noticed
- Custom **SUPERPOWER deck**: a separate deck of 4 unique attachment cards (Flexibility, Invisibility, Pyrokinesis, Rock Mimicry) set next to the encounter deck at Setup. "Reveal the top card of the SUPERPOWER deck" attaches it to Super-Skrull ("Attach to Super-Skrull"). Each villain phase (after step two), all SUPERPOWER attachments return to the deck, the deck is shuffled, and 1 card (stage I) or 2 cards (stages II and III) are revealed again. Power Multiplying, Searching for the Heir 1B and Heir to the Throne 2B also reveal from it. Power Multiplying: "If you cannot, this card gains surge" when the deck is empty.
- "SUPERPOWER." is a printed trait shown in a special bold-italic comic font (also in the text it is written in that font).
- Use of the "star" icon as a rules marker: cards with a leading star-bullet are "star" abilities; red star is used as a boost icon at bottom-right. Mt'nox/Praxagora have a small star after ATK. Pyrokinesis +1 ATK has a star; Rock Mimicry ATK is just a star.
- Boost icons: red star and pink Y. Power Multiplying explicitly defines Y as "boost icon". The Y is also used in the footer as the villain's encounter-set mark.
- **Title** attachment (Lord Kl'rt) with a **Hero Action** (hero-side rule printed with a hero icon). Lord Kl'rt is found via Heir to the Throne 2A.
- Invisible Forcefield: damage redirect shield on the highest-printed-HP enemy, max 5 damage here, then discard.
- Mt'nox: resource-type table (physical/energy/mental/wild) on discarded card from player's deck.
- New main-scheme loss condition on 2B: "If this stage is completed, the players lose the game."
- Setup contents: Super-Skrull I + II (III for expert), Super-Skrull encounter set + Standard, two modular sets: Army of Skrulls and Kree-Skrull War.
- Hit point values per player icon on villain cards (14/16/19).

## Illegible items
None. All text was legible at the used resolution. The only interpretive point: the "ATK star" badge on Rock Mimicry and the star superscripts (read at zoom) and the resource icons (identified by shape: fist, bolt, mental node).
