## Mechanics noticed
- Origin form / form change: Teddy Altman Setup puts the "origin form upgrade" into play with Skrull Origin faceup; Kree Origin / Skrull Origin are the two faces of one permanent Upgrade (numbers 2A/2B), flipped by "Action: Exhaust this card → flip this card." "Change origin form"/"change form" is a triggerable event (Alter-Ego Response heals 1; Hero Responses on Kree Durability, Kree/Skrull events).
- Form-dependent traits: the origin form grants Hulkling the KREE or SKRULL trait; many cards check "while you have the KREE/SKRULL trait" and "share that trait" (hero Response draws a card, once per round). Cards also play with "Response: After you play a card with the KREE/SKRULL trait from your hand" and "After you spend a card with the SKRULL/KREE trait" (spend = discard for resource).
- Ancestors: grants KREE or SKRULL trait to a card in hand or in play until end of round; uses "Uses (2 individual counters)" (non-standard counter wording).
- Trait-sharing with identity: encounter cards use TRAIT (special font) as a variable word meaning any trait shared with your identity/hero; "cannot change form"; "cannot ready" for KREE/SKRULL cards.
- Hero Response / Hero Interrupt / Hero Action (events usable only in hero form); Alter-Ego Action / When Revealed (Alter-Ego) / When Revealed (Hero) for forms.
- Overpay: Skrull Regeneration heals 2 per resource overpaid.
- Ignoring keyword/icon: Skrull Infiltration thwart ignores the patrol keyword and the crisis icon. Kree Ferocity: +6 ATK on basic attack, optionally 2 indirect damage → overkill. Excelsior: Restricted, basic attacks gain piercing, Resource ability. Team-Up (Hulkling and Wiccan), Max 1 per deck (Strength in Otherness). "Max 1 per player" (Pride). Identity-specific card check (Pride). Swap hand card with discard pile card (Nega Rings). Consequential damage redirected (Talos). Mur-G'nn: resource-based mill table. Confuse/stun/tough status cards used on heroes and allies; "Villainous" on nemesis minion; nemesis set is "HULKLING NEMESIS (n/5)".
- DNA Masking: side scheme that locks threat removal while no minions are in play and prevents minion damage.
- Printed numbers on cards in Aspect/Encounter folders skip some filename numbers (e.g., file 17 shows 15, file 27 shows 19); the card number is as printed.
