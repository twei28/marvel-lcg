## Mechanics noticed
---------------------------------------------------------------------
- [CORRIGE 15/10/2026 d'apres l'insert p.14 : ce sont UNE carte double face (Kly'bn / Sl'gur't), PV partages, on retourne le mechant ; ne PAS jouer deux mechants a la fois] Two co-villains in play simultaneously (Kly'bn and Sl'gur't, Villain stage I/II/III each, with different ATK/SCH). The "villain" referenced by cards (faith counters, "flip the villain", "remove 1 faith counter from the villain") seems to act as the shared pair; "flip the villain" is a recurring effect (Nogor reveal/boost, Prayer cards, Shapeless Wastes, 2A->2B When Revealed).
- New counter: **faith counter**, placed on the villains. Each villain, after step one of the villain phase, discards cards from the encounter deck equal to its faith counters and deals them out facedown to players (Kly'bn deals the non-treachery ones, Sl'gur't the treacheries). Facedown dealt cards are "facedown encounter card in player order".
- Faith counters scale Blessed by... attachments (+X SCH / +X ATK where X = faith counters on the villain) and Nogor (+1 SCH/ATK/hit point per faith counter).
- Stage III villains give themselves a tough status card on When Revealed.
- Main scheme 1 has an unusual dual advance: Forced Interrupt when last threat removed -> environment enters DESTROYED side, advance to 2B; When Completed (threat goal reached) -> environment STRONGHOLD side, advance to 2A. Stage 2A reveals Strengthen the Faith with +2 [pp] threat; 2B (starting threat 0, goal 12 [pp], +1 [pp] per round) says "If this stage is completed, the players lose the game."
- Environment "Skrull Pantheon" two-sided (Stronghold/Destroyed): Hero Action to remove a faith counter by spending 2 resources (same type on Stronghold, any type on Destroyed).
- Uses of "SKRULL" trait in bold small-caps in rules text. Keyword Hinder 2 [pp] (official keyword) on Shapeless Wastes.
- Setup mentions sets: Skrull Pantheon, Dard'van Sect, Standard, modular "Skrull Society" (not in this folder).
- Expert mode: Kly'bn II + III (and presumably Sl'gur't II + III) instead of I + II.
- Star-marked boost line ("star Boost:") appears on Blessed by cards, Nogor, Strengthen the Faith, Prayer cards: the star is a printed symbol preceding rules/boost text (and a star icon among boost icons), likely a set-specific icon/marker; its meaning is not explained on any card.

## Illegible items
None. The ambiguity about the exact meaning of the star symbol and which icon in the top-right circle vs. bottom-left on the main-scheme B sides represents threat goal vs. starting threat was inferred from standard layout.
