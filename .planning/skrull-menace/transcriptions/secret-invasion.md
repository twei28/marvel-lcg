# Secret Invasion (Skrull Menace Expansion Content) - transcription

Source folder: Scenario Packs/Secret Invasion (Skrull Menace Expansion Content)/ (MC Custom Content by Kajislav). Set label on pack cards: SECRET INVASION (n/46); footer credit "MC Custom Content By: Kajislav" plus a collector code (e.g. 189A). All text below was read from the card images. Notation: "(star)" = a black star glyph printed before an ability; "(pip)" = hollow star pips printed under SCH/ATK badges on Ally sides; "[??]" = uncertain. Italic reminder text is marked with *...*.

## Mechanics noticed
- **CAMOUFLAGED / REVEALED are traits used as flip states.** Spider-Woman (Veranke) and Impostor allies are CAMOUFLAGED; Queen Veranke and Impostor minions are REVEALED. Cards then target "the *CAMOUFLAGED* ally with the most remaining hit points", "flip", etc. No new keyword reminder text is printed for them.
- **Double-sided villain.** Spider-Woman (side A, SCH/ATK with star on ATK, cannot take damage) flips to Queen Veranke (side B). Flip triggers: Spider-Woman's Hero Action "You are a Skrull!" (exhaust a hero and spend 1 per-player resources of any type, payable as a group); Queen Veranke's Forced Response (after villain phase ends, if any player is in alter-ego form, flip; stage III adds "Otherwise, discard the top card of the encounter deck, if it is a treachery, flip"). Camouflage treachery also flips Queen Veranke / makes Spider-Woman scheme. Hit points are on the footer ("HIT POINTS 14/18/21" per player); stage I/II/III with I+II in standard, II+III in expert per the main scheme 1A contents.
- **Suspicion counters.** Spider-Woman's Forced Response places 1 suspicion counter on the "Who Do You Trust?" environment after she attacks you. That environment moves suspicion counters in lockstep with main-scheme threat: threat placed on the main scheme removes equal suspicion counters; threat removed from the main scheme adds equal suspicion counters. Main scheme 1B / 2B Forced Response: after your turn ends, if there are less than 2 (per-player) suspicion counters, put a CAMOUFLAGED ally (1B) or a REVEALED minion (2B) at random from the IMPOSTOR deck into play (ally under your control / minion engaged with you).
- **IMPOSTOR deck (20 double-sided cards).** Setup: "Create the *IMPOSTOR* deck and set it next to the encounter deck (see insert)." Side A is an Ally (CAMOUFLAGED) controlled by a player, with a Forced Interrupt "When X would leave play, flip him/her instead" (so it survives and turns into a minion). Side B is a Minion (REVEALED) with "When Revealed: Heal all damage from X and put him/her into play engaged with you" (i.e. flipping a damaged ally re-enters play as a fully-healed minion engaged with the controller). Ally pips under SCH/ATK are printed on side A only; the orange badge number appears on both sides (the card itself gives no label).
- **Impostor attachment (encounter card 7).** Attaches to a non-SKRULL ally (most remaining hit points, no Impostor attached), treats it as a SKRULL minion with a blank text box, SCH equal to its printed THW, no consequential damage, and engages its controller; otherwise the villain schemes.
- **Super-Skrull Enhancement (3 versions).** Attaches to the SKRULL minion with the most remaining hit points (or flips a CAMOUFLAGED ally and attaches to it, else surge); gives +2 hit points / retaliate 1 / (no extra effect on the +3 version). Boost: "Reveal this card, after this activation ends."
- **Main scheme.** 1B threat goal 8 per player, acceleration +1 per player (starred), starting threat 2 per player, adds 1 threat per SKRULL enemy and IMPOSTOR ally in play. 2A "When Revealed" finds/reveals Mistrust and Paranoia or removes 2 per-player suspicion counters; 2B acceleration "+X" where X = twice the number of SKRULL enemies in play, goal 14 per player, starting threat 1 per player, stage completion loses the game.
- **Side schemes** (printed landscape): Chaos on the Battlefield (starting threat 1, Incite 2, + SCH of all IMPOSTOR characters), Fulfill the Prophecy (starting threat 3, Hinder 1 per-player, SKRULL minions gain guard and patrol), Mistrust and Paranoia (starting threat 2, no attacking/defending vs other players' minions, no group payment of costs).
- **Keywords used** on Impostor minions: Guard, Toughness, Patrol, Retaliate 1, Quickstrike, Villainous, Incite 3 (all with reminder text on the card except Retaliate 1 and Quickstrike).
- **Printing quirks to note:** Camouflage "Remove 3 suspicion counter" (singular "counter" as printed); Spider-Man (Ch'Gra) minion "When Revealed" reads "Heal all damage from Hagar and put him into play" (copy error from Thor); card 28 flavor "I Speak Skrull perfectly well." (capital S); Captain Marvel (Khn'nr) is "Captain Marvel" with "him" though art is Mar-Vell. Impostor encounter set card counts: n/46 numbering covers setup + encounter + impostor cards; collector codes 189A-227B.
