# Skrull Menace - Campaign cards (transcription)

Source: Campaigns/Skrull Menace (Released)/. Footer on every card: "MC Custom Content By: Kajislav" + number. Set label shown: "<AFFINITY> / CAMPAIGN" bottom right (campaign cards, numbers 263-288). Unique star = the four-point star printed before the name (written "unique" below). The bottom-left corner of each card carries a small colored resource-type icon (written as "resource icon"). Allies/supports/upgrades show no hit points on the image.

Format notes: "->" stands for the printed arrow. "(bullet star)" = the solid black star glyph at start of a rules line (the "star" ability marker, as printed on ally text).

## Mechanics noticed
- Trait *SUPERPOWER* (Super-Skrull, Searching for Powers): cards with that trait are attached facedown to Super-Skrull (max 4); Super-Skrull gets +1 THW/+1 ATK per different SUPERPOWER card attached.
- Facedown attachment under an ally: Paibok attaches discarded treachery cards (max 2), gets +1 THW/ATK per treachery attached.
- Ally treated as minion: War Skrull / "Chilly McHotpants" is an Ally with SCH instead of THW, treated as a minion engaged with you.
- Side-scheme "When Defeated" payoffs (player side schemes with Victory 0); Buddy Training uses X = villain's stage number and "tough status card".
- Fickle Alliance: take an encounter minion under your control as a blank-text ally using its SCH as THW, with consequential damage.
- Skrull Military Training: threat removable only by a SKRULL ally; grants +1 THW/ATK/DEF from victory display.
- "Alliance." keyword on Princess Anelle (cost X = players + SKRULL enemies in play); remove from game -> skip the villain phase.
- Keyword "Initiative." (Crusader); "Temporary." (Inhibitor Mask, Ultimate Nullifier); "Toughness." (Paibok).
- "Hero Resource" ability type (Skrull Battleship) -> generate a Wild resource for any player; "Hero Action"/"Hero Response" used by supports; Xavin refers to events with "Hero Action".
- "Play under any player's control" on upgrades (Reverting Device, Skrull Shapeshifter, Sy-Torak Gem) and "Attach to a non-SKRULL ally"/"Attach to a SKRULL enemy" upgrades.
- Ultimate Nullifier: attached to the villain it gains a hazard icon (inline per-player icon + hazard glyph).
- Sy-Torak Gem: "Forced Interrupt: When your deck empties" spend a resource or discard (new hit point modifier "+3 hit points").
- Skrull Shapeshifter: identity gains SKRULL trait; Interrupt sets hit point dial to 1.
- Cooperation Progress Reminder: campaign-mode interrupt placing defeated minion/side scheme into the victory display (cost: discard top card of deck); double-sided reminder.
- Deadpool gag cards ('Pool aspect, taped labels): Pool-O-Vision counts four inline icons in play (draw max 3).
