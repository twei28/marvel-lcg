# Guide campagne 2026 : données attestées et points d’intégration

Les règles ci-dessous sont extraites du livret 2026, p. 2 à 7 du PDF et des images prioritaires. Les identifiants `qx` proviennent de notre correspondance, **pas** de l’éditeur.

## Scénarios

### 1. The Big Apple
Pages PDF : 4, 5.
**Mise en place:**
- Choose three of eight CAPO villains; side A normal, side B expert; set others aside
- Main schemes Gang War, Silence the City, Gunrunner Warehouse
- Encounter set The Big Apple and Standard sets
- Shuffle 9 LEADS cards
- 9 locations in 3x3; one capo row; players start Midtown
- Two modular sets Cops and Tracksuit Mafia (FNE) in campaign mode
- Optional Uptown Expansion: add 3 top locations, 3 Uptown leads, four CAPOs rather than three
**Victoire:**
- Record Gunrunner identity
- Put Seized Tech Crate (campaign card 1 reverse) into play for scenarios 2 and 3
**Comportements spécifiques:**
- spatial grid
- separate turf per villain
- location-filtered effects
- main-scheme win progression
- separate leads deck

### 2. Hobgoblin / Jigsaw
Pages PDF : 6.
**Mise en place:**
- Choose Hobgoblin if Gunrunner was Count Nefaria, Mr. Negative, Nightshade or Ringmaster; else Jigsaw (other four CAPOs)
- Choose main scheme based on location where final lead was discovered in Big Apple (printed lookup table on campaign card 6133A)
- Modular sets Punisher's Hit List, The Syndicate, Capo of the Past
**Victoire:**
- Place Punisher's Ordnance Cache (back of campaign card 2) in play for scenario 3
**Comportements spécifiques:**
- dynamic villain choice
- dynamic main-scheme choice
- Punisher punishment targeting
- Pumpkin Bomb boost reveal

### 3. Punishment
Pages PDF : 7, 8.
**Mise en place:**
- Punisher villain stages I/II standard or II/III expert
- One MC/DC UNDERLING (Hobgoblin or Jigsaw) NOT fought in campaign battle 2; Underling uses villain I standard and II expert
- Main schemes One Last Kill and Escaping Punishment
- Reveal Justice or Punishment environment
- Punisher's Armory modular set recommended and required in campaign mode
- Put campaign card 3 into play and flip it when Punisher's first stage is defeated
**Victoire:**
- Find Punisher and put him into play under any player control for scenario 4
**Défaite:**
- If underling escapes by completing main scheme 2B, campaign scenario lost even if Punisher could still be defeated
**Comportements spécifiques:**
- two villains in play
- target lock focused/distracted
- punisher targeting minions and underling
- underling threat accumulation
- campaign-dependent underling HP

### 4. Kingsfall
Pages PDF : 8.
**Mise en place:**
- Epic multiplayer Kingpin variant (up to 4 game pods)
- Kingpin with Long Live environment
- SWAT Team and Tracksuit Mafia modular sets (FNE)
- Pod public support and Kingsfall-specific scenario cards
**Victoire:**
- Each pod defeats Kingpin to place damage equal to its player count on The King
- If shared The King damage >= threat, pod wins
**Comportements spécifiques:**
- epic multiplayer across pods
- shared public support and The King
- Kingpin stage advancement between pods
- fallback for single table to be decided

## FAQ imprimée

- PDF p.5: Movement action may be taken multiple times per round if each cost paid
- PDF p.5: Minions attached to locations cannot be engaged from other locations in same turf
- PDF p.5: Teamwork in this encounter set checks for other minions in play and is not limited to turf
- PDF p.5: Unoccupied location = location without a player present
- PDF p.5: Limit once per round on a location applies globally, not once per player
- PDF p.6: Punisher targets enemy with fewest remaining HP who can be attacked; if the lowest-HP enemy cannot be attacked, he targets the next-lowest; he cannot attack himself (PDF p.6)
- PDF p.6: Punisher defeats minion or friendly character triggers Punisher effects but When Defeated addressed to player does not trigger
- PDF p.8: Underling HP = printed numeric HP ignoring per-player icon, plus 5 HP per player (from In the Crosshairs)
- PDF p.8: Kingsfall Long Live moves to next villain stage at end of player phase if pod public support requirement reached
