VILLAIN CARDS
VILLAIN CARDS
Each of the ﬁve new scenarios in this expansion features 
a new villain: Vermin, Scorpion, The Lizard, Vulture, and 
Kraven the Hunter. Each scenario has its own section in this 
rulebook.
IDENTITY CARDS
IDENTITY CARDS
There are no new heroes included in this expansion, but I 
recommend using animal-themed heroes such as Spider-
Man, Spider-Woman, Black Panther, Ant-Man or Wasp, to 
truly immerse yourself in the hunting theme.
COMPONENTS
COMPONENTS
226 cards, consisting of 57 player cards, 15 villain cards, 
138 encounter cards, and 16 trap cards.
NEW MODE OF PLAY
NEW MODE OF PLAY
Each of the scenarios in this custom expansion can be 
played individually as standalone adventures, or as part of 
one epic campaign.
CAMPAIGN MODE RULES
CAMPAIGN MODE RULES
Campaign mode combines all ﬁve scenarios in the Hunting 
Season expansion into one epic experience where the 
outcome of each game impacts the next scenario. To 
complete the campaign, the players must win all ﬁve 
scenarios in numerical order, starting with scenario #1 – 
Vermin and ending with scenario #5 – Kraven the Hunter.
To begin a campaign, the players start by choosing their 
heroes. Each player must use their chosen identity for 
the entire campaign, but they are free to change aspects 
and alter the contents of their deck following the deck 
customization rules in the Marvel Champions Rules 
Reference between scenarios.
To play a scenario in campaign mode, set up the scenario 
as per the normal rules of the game. Then, follow that 
scenario’s setup instructions in the order in which they are 
listed in the Campaign Instructions.
When the game ends, if the players won, follow that 
scenario’s victory instructions in the order in which they 
are listed in the Campaign Instructions. If the players lost, 
follow that scenario’s loss instructions in the order in which 
they are listed in the Campaign Instructions.
CAMPAIGN LOG
CAMPAIGN LOG
The campaign log found on the last page of this rulebook 
tracks the progress of the entire campaign. At the end of 
each scenario, the players record their results by entering 
the required information in the campaign log.
CARDS ADDED TO DECKS
CARDS ADDED TO DECKS
During a campaign, cards can be added to players’ decks 
as part of a scenario’s victory instructions, or in between 
scenarios in The Lodge (see page 3). When a card is added 
to a player’s deck this way, write that card’s title in the 
matching ﬁeld of the campaign log.
Added cards must be included in the player’s deck for the 
rest of the campaign. Cards added to the deck as part of 
a campaign do not count toward a player’s minimum or 
maximum deck size.
Cards that were created speciﬁcally for use in the Hunting 
Season campaign cannot be included in any player’s deck 
unless they are playing that campaign and the players 
were directed to add them to their decks by the Campaign 
Instructions.
EXPERT CAMPAIGN
EXPERT CAMPAIGN
For players who want a more challenging campaign 
experience, The Hunting Season expansion comes with 
everything you need to play an expert campaign. The rules 
for this alternate play format are found on page 11.
 Some setup, victory and loss instructions are 
preceded by Expert Campaign Only. Players should

EXPERIENCE
EXPERIENCE
During a campaign, in a scenario’s victory and 
loss instructions, players will earn Experience 
based on their performance. This symbolizes that 
hero’s experience with hunting and the wild. A 
player’s current number of Experience must be 
written down in the campaign log.
THE LODGE
THE LODGE
Between each scenario, players get the option to 
add cards to their deck from The Lodge. These 
cards have the text “Experience Cost” followed 
by a number. This number states how much 
Experience a player must spend to add that card 
to their deck. This act symbolizes the hero using 
their hunting experience to gain new skills, allies 
and items. After spending their Experience, that 
player must write down in the campaign log how 
much Experience they have left.
The cards available in The Lodge at the start of 
the campaign is: Rocket Raccoon (x1), Old Man 
Logan (x1), Black Cat (x1), Ka-Zar (x1), Evade 
(x3), Taunt (x3), Steadfast (x3), Finish Them 
Off (x3), Fresh Tracks (x3), Savage Land (x1), 
Hunting Riﬂe (x3), Hatchet (x3), Hunterbot (x3),

Gibbon (x1) is set aside and cannot be added to 
any player’s deck until the campaign instructs the 
card to be added to The Lodge.
THE MASTER HUNTER
THE MASTER HUNTER
At the start of the campaign the players decide 
on which player to give the title of Master Hunter. 
If the players are unable to decide, the title goes 
to the player who last met an untamed animal 
in the wild. The Master Hunter will be instructed 
to make decisions or decide ties during the 
campaign by the campaign instructions of some 
scenarios.

TRAP DECK
TRAP DECK
Kraven the Hunter starts the game with a special “trap” 
deck. To create the trap deck, shufﬂe all 16 of Kraven the 
Hunter’s trap cards together (Ferocious Lion (x2), Fierce 
Cheetah (x2), Bear Trap (x2), Capture Net (x2), Quicksand 
(x2), Tranquilizer Darts (x3), Swinging Boulder (x3)). Then, 
place the trap deck face down next to the encounter deck.
During the game, trap cards will be dealt face down from 
the trap deck. When trap cards are revealed and then 
discarded, they are placed in a special discard pile that 
belongs to the trap deck. If the trap deck is ever empty, 
shufﬂe the trap discard pile back into the trap deck. There 
is no penalty for doing this.
RULES CLARIFICATIONS
RULES CLARIFICATIONS
Q. When you deal yourself a trap card, do you reveal 
it as well?
A. You only reveal your trap cards when you are instructed 
to. In this scenario, only the “Forced Interrupt”-effect on 
Kraven the Hunter will instruct you to reveal the trap cards 
that have been dealt to you.
Q What happens to the enemy that would be defeated 
in Chameleon’s “Forced Interrupt”-effect?
A. The instance of damage that would defeat the enemy is 
healed from that enemy.
Q. What happens when you’re instructed to deal 
yourself a trap card, but there are no trap cards left in 
the deck and discard pile?
A. Nothing happens. If you cannot resolve the text, then 
you ignore that text.
Q. Do trap cards count as encounter cards for the 
purpose of effects that refer to encounter cards?
A. No, trap cards do not count as encounter cards.

EXPERT CAMPAIGN RULES
EXPERT CAMPAIGN RULES
For players who want a more challenging campaign 
experience, the Hunting Season custom expansion comes 
with everything you need to play an expert campaign.
EXPERT CAMPAIGN SET
EXPERT CAMPAIGN SET
There are four encounter sets labeled “Expert Campaign 
Set.” Each set contains the same four encounter cards with 
player-card backs. These cards can be added to a player’s 
deck during a campaign.
At the beginning of a campaign, each player is assigned 
a number from 1 to 4 in the campaign log. Each Expert 
Campaign Set is also numbered from 1 to 4. When a player 
is instructed to add a card from their expert-encounter set 
to their deck, they must take that card from the set that 
matches their player number.
PERSISTENT DAMAGE
PERSISTENT DAMAGE
While playing the Hunting Season campaign at the expert 
level, each player must record their remaining hit points in 
the campaign log after they win a game. This determines 
each player’s starting hit points for the next scenario. If a 
player’s remaining hit point value is higher than their base 
hit point value, record their base hit points in the campaign 
log instead. The setup instructions for each scenario offer 
players the opportunity to restore their identity to their 
full hit point value by adding an encounter card from their 
expert-campaign set to their deck.
If a player is defeated during a scenario that their 
teammates go on to win, they can rejoin their teammates 
for the next scenario by adding an encounter card to their 
deck during setup to restore their identity to full hit points.
ENCOUNTER CARDS  
ENCOUNTER CARDS  
IN PLAYER DECKS
IN PLAYER DECKS
The encounter cards in the expert campaign sets have 
player-card backs because they are meant to be added to 
player decks, but they are still encounter cards.
When a player draws an encounter card from their deck, 
they must immediately put that card into play in their play 
area. They do not draw a card to replace that encounter 
card.
These encounter cards represent the consequences of 
a player’s choices. As a result, only the player with the 
encounter card in their play area can use their abilities to 
deal with that card.
HUNTER MODE ENVIRONMENTS
HUNTER MODE ENVIRONMENTS
When starting an expert campaign, the players can choose 
to play on Hunter Mode. Playing on Hunter Mode counts 
as playing an expert campaign and therefore follows all 
the rules of an expert campaign, with the addition of an 
environment for each scenario. A named environment is 
put into play at the setup of each scenario if the players are 
playing in Hunter Mode, and increases the difﬁculty of the 
scenario considerably. To get the highest-level difﬁculty 
for this campaign without playing the scenarios on heroic 
difﬁculty, the players should play an expert campaign on 
Hunter Mode and against each scenario on expert.
